Features

Everything you need. Nothing that spies on you.

A browser built for your thumb, with privacy in plain sight and details that feel good.

Lince Rules

On-device ad and tracker blocking, plus Privacy X-Ray to see what was stopped.

Strict private mode

Private tabs, history, permissions and reading never reach persistent storage.

Lince Sync

Sync end-to-end encrypted tabs through your own server. Your keys, not ours.

Profiles and Lince Stacks

Keep home, work and study apart, each with its own lynx avatar, and bundle related tabs.

Reader

Clean reading, type that suits you, offline saving and text-to-speech.

Lince Trails

See where every link led and open any branch of the path in a new tab.

Site Capsules

Pin a site to your home screen with its own icon, profile and focused interface.

Liquid metal and Sunset

An animated loading border on the address bar and a sunset backdrop while you switch tabs.
