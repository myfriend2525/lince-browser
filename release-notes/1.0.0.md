# Welcome to Lince Browser 1.0

Lince Browser is a gesture-first, privacy-focused browser for Android. Version 1.0 introduces the new
Lince identity together with smoother loading feedback, more reliable downloads, and better
compatibility with modern websites.

## A new look

- A new lynx app icon, with matching themed icon support on Android 13 and later.
- Twenty-four lynx avatars for your profiles. Pick one when you create a profile, or long-press a
  profile to change it.
- A warm sunset backdrop behind your tabs while you switch between them.

## Liquid metal loading

The address bar now shows an animated liquid-metal border while a page loads. You can switch back to
the rainbow or tonal styles under **Settings → Appearance → Address-bar loading indicator**.

## More reliable downloads

- Download notifications update smoothly and always finish with a clear **Download complete** state.
- Private-tab downloads in the System WebView edition no longer pass through Android's download
  manager, so private cookies and addresses are never stored by the system.
- Starting a download no longer briefly blocks the browser.

## Better website compatibility

Fixed headers on sites that animate their position no longer drift down the page while you browse.

## Choose your engine

Lince Browser is available with GeckoView, which supports Firefox extensions and encrypted DNS, and
as a lightweight System WebView edition.
